Questions, answered

What is GEO, and why does my business need it?
GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is about getting your business named in AI-generated answers — ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. More customers now ask AI tools “who is the best…?” before they ever click a website. GEO makes sure the answer includes you: clear service pages, consistent business details everywhere, helpful FAQs and content AI can confidently cite.
How long does SEO take to show results?
Technical fixes and Google Business Profile improvements can show movement within a few weeks. Competitive keyword rankings typically build over 3–6 months of consistent work. Anyone promising page one overnight is selling you a risk, not a result — I’ll always give you a realistic timeline for your market.
Do you work with small businesses?
Yes — small and local businesses are exactly who I work with: movers, clinics, salons, real estate agencies, home services and similar companies across the UAE. You don’t need a big budget to start; a proper audit and the right fixes come first.
What does your SEO audit include?
A full check of your website’s technical health, current Google rankings for money keywords, on-page SEO, content gaps versus competitors, Google Business Profile, and how visible you are in AI search results — delivered as a prioritized action list in plain English, not a 60-page PDF of jargon.
